Known limitations

  • Flow-matching TTS evasion (#252): Flow-matching text-to-speech systems (Chatterbox, F5-TTS) produce audio that this model frequently scores as bona fide. A low risk score does not rule out synthetic speech from these generators.
  • Channel confounds (#253): Bona fide speech recorded over low-quality channels (VoIP, telephony, heavy compression) can produce elevated false-positive rates. Scores are most reliable on clean, studio-quality or near-field recordings.
